The Arcade

Curiosity is sufficient justification.

More than winning

Every game involves decisions. Some are obvious, some depend on chance, and some only make sense after you've played a few rounds. In this arcade, you'll face computer opponents that make decisions according to different strategies.

Here's the catch: the opponents won't tell you how they think. Your job is to play, pay attention, look for patterns, and develop explanations that you can test.

You don't need to know how to program. You do need curiosity, a willingness to experiment, and a healthy suspicion of your first guess.

Play like an investigator

  1. Observe. What does the computer do in different situations?
  2. Hypothesize. What rule might explain those choices?
  3. Experiment. Change the starting conditions or your own moves. Does your explanation still hold?
  4. Reflect. When does a strategy succeed? When does it fail? What evidence supports your conclusion?

Winning is fun. Understanding why is the real challenge.
