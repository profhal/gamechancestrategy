Bold Claims

Ten dice. Hidden hands. How much do you believe?

Rules

You and the computer each roll five six-sided dice. You can see your own dice, but not your opponent's. Players alternate making claims about how many dice of one face appear across both hands.

Every new claim must increase the number of dice claimed, or keep that number and increase the face value. Instead of raising, you may CALL the previous claim. If at least that many matching dice exist, the bidder wins; otherwise the caller wins. A round ends on the first call.

Example: “3 fours” claims at least three of the ten dice show four. “3 fives” or “4 ones” are legal raises; “2 sixes” is not.
