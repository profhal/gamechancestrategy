Tic-Tac-Toe

Games, Chance, and Strategy · An experiment in anticipating moves

Rules

Players alternate placing X and O on a 3×3 board. Three of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins. A full board without a winner is a draw.

New game

Changing settings takes effect when starting a new game or playing a custom position.

Play

Click a square to cycle through Empty → X → O. For an ordinary reachable position, X has played the same number of turns as O, or one more.

Move history

    Watch the computer's choices. Can you work out how its opponents differ? Their strategies are not revealed during play.
