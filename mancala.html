How to play

Each player owns six pits and a store. On your turn, choose one of your nonempty pits. Its stones are distributed counterclockwise, one per pit, including your store but skipping your opponent's store. If your last stone lands in your store, take another turn. If it lands in an empty pit on your side, capture that stone and all stones in the opposite pit, provided the opposite pit contains stones. When one player's six pits are empty, the other player moves their remaining stones into their store. The player with more stones wins.

To help investigate the computer's strategy, the move history records which pit was chosen and what happened, but does not explain why.
