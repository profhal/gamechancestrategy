Rules

Each fleet contains five ships (lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, and 2). Take turns firing at untried squares. A hit is marked; when every square of a ship has been hit, it sinks. Sink the opposing fleet to win. Ships cannot overlap, but they may touch.
