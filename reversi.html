Reversi

Games, Chance, and Strategy · An experiment in positional play

Rules

Place a disc to enclose one or more opposing discs in a straight line, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Enclosed discs flip to your color. If you have no legal move, you pass. The game ends when neither player can move. The player with more discs wins.

New game

Settings take effect when you start a new game. The computer's strategy is not revealed during play.

Click a square repeatedly to cycle through empty, black, and white. The editor accepts any arrangement with at least one disc; it does not verify that a position could arise from legal play.
