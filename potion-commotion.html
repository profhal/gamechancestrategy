Potion Commotion

Two apprentice wizards. Fourteen days. One Golden Cauldron.

Rules

Two apprentice wizards share two to six enchanted cauldrons before their final examination. Each day, both apprentices take one action. Potions change automatically at the end of each day. Bottle potions to collect their magical potency. The apprentice with the highest bottled total wins the Golden Cauldron!

Healthy growth: 1 → 3 → 6 → 10 → 15. Hexed growth: +1 per day. After reaching 15, every potion decays: 15 → 10 → 6 → 3 → 1 → 0. At zero, it evaporates. ↑ means growing, ★ means peak, ↓ means irreversible decay. The potion bottle shows potency from 0 to 15; its liquid color also distinguishes rising, falling, and hexed potions.

Actions and contamination rules
ActionWhen available
StartAny empty cauldron
BottleYour unhexed potion, growing or decaying
HexOpponent's unhexed potion, including decaying
DispelYour hexed potion

Hexed potions cannot be bottled. Dispelling uses your action; bottling must wait until another turn. A hex slows growth to +1 per day, but never stops decay. Decaying potions can be hexed. Dispelling removes the hex; it does not reverse decay.
