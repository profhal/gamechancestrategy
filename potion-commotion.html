🧪 Potion Commotion

Grow your potions, protect your cauldrons, and know when to bottle.

How to play

Over 14 days, claim cauldrons, brew potions, and bottle them for points. Potency grows to 15, then decays. A HEX slows an opponent's brewing; DISPEL removes a HEX from your own cauldron. You may own up to three cauldrons, and after casting HEX you must wait two of your turns before casting another. Each player takes one action per day; the cauldrons brew after the second player's action.
