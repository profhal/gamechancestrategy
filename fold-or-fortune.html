Fold or Fortune

Five cards. Two choices. One showdown.

Rules

Five cards are dealt face down. You reveal one, then the computer reveals one. Both secretly choose FOLD or PLAY.

If both fold, nobody scores. If only one plays, that player earns 1 point. If both play, each reveals a second card; the higher two-card total earns 2 points. Ties earn nothing.

Aces count as 1; Jacks, Queens, and Kings count as 10. Suits don't matter. Losing a showdown currently carries no penalty.
